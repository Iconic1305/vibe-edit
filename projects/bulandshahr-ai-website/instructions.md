# Editing brief: Bulandshahr viral clothing store website (2 AI tools)

## Video
- Raw footage: shared album https://photos.icloud.com/shared/album/020PQnNCif65_qygR_HHS_MJg
  (NOT yet in this project: the cloud sandbox blocked photos.icloud.com. Put the files in `input/`.)
- Topic: I use 2 AI tools to build a website for Bulandshahr's viral clothing store.
- Format: 9:16 (assumed: Reels/Shorts; change in edit.json + project.json if it's for YouTube)
- Language of speech: Hindi/Hinglish.

## Style
- Professional, punchy, fast-paced; motion graphics on every key beat (tool names, steps, results).
- Colours: accent #FFD400 on white captions, dark UI cards (adjust once we see the footage/brand).

## Captions (Hinglish, Roman script, e.g. "Aaj hum ek website banayenge")
- Creative, word by word, 2-3 words per group, key word highlighted in the accent colour.
- **Layered depth:** some words appear BEHIND me (the speaker), some IN FRONT, to raise retention.
  Needs a clean cut-out of me (background removal). Behind-words go between the footage and my cut-out.
- Spelling of names (see names.txt): the 2 AI tool names, Bulandshahr, store name.

## Motion graphics
- Title/hook card at the start; lower third with my name/handle.
- Tool name cards that pop up when I name each AI tool (logos if I provide them in `assets/`).
- Step counter / progress for "tool 1 -> tool 2 -> website", website screenshot/screen-recording reveal.
- Zoom punch-ins on key words; sound effects on every effect.

## Sound
- Background music under my voice, low volume (needs a music file I have rights to in `music/`).

## Don'ts
- Nothing in the bottom 20% or near the right edge (app buttons).
